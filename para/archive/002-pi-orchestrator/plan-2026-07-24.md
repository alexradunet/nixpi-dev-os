## Why this matters

Worker orchestration currently runs through herdr, an external terminal-multiplexer daemon pinned in the flake, spoken to over a version-pinned socket protocol (protocol 17). Roughly half of the 400-line bridge extension (`pi/herdr-agents/index.ts`) exists only to cope with that daemon being external: identity pinning, replaced-handle recovery, uncertain-submission phases, capability negotiation. Replacing it with a patched copy of pi's official `subagent` example (one-shot `pi` subprocesses) deletes the daemon, the protocol, and all the defensive code, and leaves workers as pure functions: brief in, artifact out. The grill artifact records the full decision rationale.
